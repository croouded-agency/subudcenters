Interactive World Map — Wireflow for Approval (SubudCenters.com)

Purpose: confirm the committee’s shared understanding of the interaction flow before scope/timeline/quotation updates. This is intentionally low-fidelity (wireflow) to avoid design discussions.
Wireflow-only • Not visual design
1) Assumptions to Confirm Please reply: Confirm / Changes needed
  • Single “World Map” experience (one page/experience; not separate pages per zone/country/city).
  • Map starts at full world view with standard zoom in/out (actual zoom UI handled by the map library).
  • Filter order: Zone → Country → City/Region.
  • Selecting a Zone filters available countries.
  • Selecting a Country shows clickable pins/dots for centers in that country and filters City/Region.
  • Selecting a City/Region shows a list of Subud centers in that location.
  • Clicking a pin or a center in the list opens the individual center page.
  • Pins are auto-generated from the approved center database using stored latitude/longitude.
  • No manual pin entry on the map.
  • “City/Region” uses existing database values (no boundary polygons).
  • No zone/country shape highlighting is assumed in Phase 1 — filtering only.
