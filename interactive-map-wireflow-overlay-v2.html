1) Assumptions to Confirm Please reply: Confirm / Changes needed
  • Single “World Map” experience (one page; not separate pages per zone/country/city).
  • Map starts at full world view with standard zoom/pan (map library handles zoom UI).
  • Filter order: Zone → Country → City/Region.
  • Selecting a Zone filters available countries (via map click or dropdown).
  • Selecting a Country shows clickable pins/dots for centers in that country and filters City/Region.
  • Selecting a City/Region shows a list of Subud centers in that location.
  • Clicking a pin or a center in the list opens the individual center page.
  • Pins are auto-generated from the approved center database using stored latitude/longitude.
  • No manual pin entry on the map itself.
  • “City/Region” uses existing database values (no administrative boundary polygons).
  • No zone/country shape highlighting is required for this approval; dummy Zone bubbles demonstrate “click a Zone”.
